My Journey

I am Teacher Fahadh, a technical educator with a deep passion for unlocking the potential of technology in the classroom. Based in Hargeisa, Somaliland, I have dedicated my career to ensuring that students have access to the critical computer skills required in today's global economy.

Specializing in the Pearson Edexcel curriculum, I bring a practical, hands-on approach to ICT education. I believe that learning computer science is not just about understanding software, but about developing the problem-solving mindset necessary for the modern workforce.

Throughout my years of teaching, I have focused on making complex technical concepts accessible and engaging, fostering an environment where curiosity and digital proficiency go hand-in-hand.
